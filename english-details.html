Chihuahuan Desert
← Back
The Chihuahuan Desert is a warm desert located in southwestern North America. It is the largest hot desert in North America, with a landscape featuring arid mountains, desert basins, and sparse vegetation adapted to extreme heat and drought conditions.

Texas Blackland Prairies
← Back
The Texas Blackland Prairies ecoregion is a fertile prairie region characterized by dark, nutrient-rich clay soils formed from limestone parent material. This region historically supported tallgrass prairie vegetation and is highly productive for agriculture. Today it features remnant prairies with diverse wildflower and native grass species.
